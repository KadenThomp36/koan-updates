Koan support

Koan is a web browser for iPhone and Mac that keeps your tabs in workspaces.

Help in the app

  • Tabs and workspaces: tap the tabs button at the right of the address bar, or swipe up on the address bar. Swipe across the sidebar's header to change workspace.
  • Glance: touch and hold a link, then tap the preview.
  • Sync with your Mac: Settings ▸ Sync on the iPhone and in Koan on the Mac, signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Private tabs: open the sidebar and tap Private.
  • Settings: the ••• button in the address bar ▸ Settings.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Say which iPhone you have and what you expected to happen.

Privacy

Koan collects no data about you. Read the privacy policy.
