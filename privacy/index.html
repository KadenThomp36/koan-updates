Koan Privacy Policy

Koan is a web browser for iPhone and Mac, made by KDENTEE LLC. This policy covers Koan for iPhone. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

We don't collect any data about you. Koan has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that reports anywhere. What you do in Koan stays on your iPhone, and, only if you turn sync on, in your own iCloud account, where we can't read it.

What Koan keeps on your iPhone

Your open tabs, workspaces, pinned tabs and folders, bookmarks, history, downloads, small pictures of your tabs, site icons, and your settings. They live in Koan's own storage on your iPhone, which no other app can read. Websites keep their own cookies and data in WebKit's storage, as they do in Safari. Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Clear History and Website Data removes history and website data; deleting the app removes everything.

Private tabs leave nothing behind: they are never saved, never in history, never synced, and their website data is erased when the last private tab closes.

What Koan sends, and to whom

Koan sends nothing about you to us. On its own it makes only these requests:

  • Site icons, fetched from the site you just visited, at the address that site gives.
  • Search suggestions, only if you turn them on in Settings, sent to the search engine you chose, never from a private tab.
  • Sync, only if you turn it on: your workspaces, pinned tabs, folders, bookmarks and, if you choose, history and a list of your open tabs, sent to your own iCloud account's private database through Apple's CloudKit. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; we have no access to it. Private tabs, passwords and website data never sync. Turning sync off stops it; Settings ▸ Sync ▸ Reset Sync Data deletes what's in iCloud.

Everything else Koan loads is the web pages you open, which those sites receive as they would in any browser. Koan blocks known ads and trackers by default, with lists that ship inside the app.

Permissions

A website can use the camera, microphone or your location only after you allow it, as in Safari. Koan saves an image to your photos only when you choose Save to Photos. Face ID is used only if you turn on Lock Private Tabs; Koan learns only whether it succeeded.

Children

Koan is a general web browser and isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date, and the app will show it in Settings ▸ About Koan. Questions: see the support page linked from Koan's App Store page.
